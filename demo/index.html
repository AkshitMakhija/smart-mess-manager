Smart Mess Manager

AI-Assisted Mess Monitoring & Student Interaction System

Smart Mess Manager is an intelligent system designed to enhance the dining experience at university mess facilities. By leveraging AI-powered crowd estimation and predictive analytics, the system helps students make informed decisions about meal timing while providing administrators with valuable insights for better resource management.
